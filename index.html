Decoding HCM — HCM Typing Test & Practice Platform

Welcome to Decoding HCM, a typing practice platform for candidates preparing for HCM typing tests. Practise typing passages, review your speed and accuracy, and use the Self Test tool to paste your own content and practise at your preferred pace.

Practise typing and track your progress

Use our HCM typing test practice to explore available test categories, or open Decoding HCM Self Test to practise with your own passage. You can also visit the typing practice PDF and study material library.

What is Decoding HCM?

Decoding HCM is an independent online typing practice website designed to help users build typing speed, improve accuracy and review typing mistakes. Practice scores are for preparation and learning; check the relevant recruitment notification for official test rules and qualifying criteria.
