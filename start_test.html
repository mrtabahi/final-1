BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Your test is ready

1. Scoring: After submission, review your result with either the BSF practice formula or the CRPF positional practice formula. The selected formula and its deductions will be shown clearly.

2. Focus: Keep this tab active during the test. Switching tabs may interrupt your concentration.

3. Typing controls: Paste is disabled. Backspace is controlled by the test settings. Use the toolbar to adjust supported display options.

4. Timer: The clock starts with your first keystroke. You can submit early, or the test will submit automatically when time expires. The result uses the measured attempt time.
